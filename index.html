Haolan Xu

许皓岚

Hi! I'm currently a visiting scholar at Michigan State University, working with Prof. Xiaoming Liu. Previously, I was a visiting student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Narendra Ahuja. Before that, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Florida, working with Prof. Jorg Peters, and my Bachelor's degree from Sichuan University.

My research focuses on human motion modeling — learning dense, human-centric motion representations from video, moving beyond generic scene perception toward explicit human-aware modeling. I am particularly interested in:

  • Structural priors — how the human body is shaped and connected
  • Motion dynamics — how the body moves under physical laws
  • Behavior semantics — what intent, action, or identity the motion represents
  • Human–object interaction — how humans physically contact and engage with the world

Outside of work, I love traveling, the NBA, and F1.
